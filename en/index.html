What is TBS

TBS connects spirits brands with professional buyers in Cartagena. It serves both on-trade and off-trade channels through three engagement models that can be adapted to each operation.

On-Trade Channel

Hotels, restaurants, bars, nightclubs, clubs, gastropubs, events, and casinos. Availability, speed, activations, and service experience are key.

Off-Trade Channel

Liquor stores, supermarkets, minimarkets, specialty stores, and convenience stores in Cartagena. Coverage, visibility, and delivery performance.

Three clear models

From selling with the Brand’s own fulfillment to operating Brand inventory, logistics, billing, receivables, and data. Each model defines responsibilities clearly.

Model details

How does
each model work?

What is included, what is excluded, and each party’s responsibilities — with no fine print.

Model 01

Dropshipping by TBS

TBS sells, invoices, and collects. The Brand retains inventory and ships directly to the customer from its own operation.

✓ Included
  • Listing on the TBS platform and commercial portfolio
  • Commercial management through the platform and sales force
  • Order receipt and transmission to the Brand
  • Customer collections and receivables management
  • TBS electronic invoicing to the end customer
  • Monthly settlement of documented sales
✗ Not included
  • Storage at TBS
  • Picking, packing, or order preparation by TBS
  • Transportation by TBS
  • Responsibility for Brand stockouts
  • Responsibility for damage during Brand-managed transportation
  • Activations or trade marketing (additional service)
TBS is responsible for
  • Listing and commercially promoting the product
  • Receiving and communicating the order to the Brand
  • Collecting and managing customer receivables
  • Issuing electronic invoices to the end customer
  • Settling the Brand for documented and accepted orders
Brand is responsible for
  • Maintaining accurate and up-to-date availability
  • Confirming or rejecting each order promptly
  • Delivering orders complete, in good condition, and on time
  • Submitting delivery notes or proof of delivery to TBS
  • Issuing an invoice to TBS for delivered product
  • Complying with legal, tax, and departmental excise requirements
Key policy: If the Brand confirms availability and then fails to ship all or part of the order due to a stockout or inventory error, TBS may charge a commercial management fee equivalent to the commission that would have been earned on the undelivered value.
Model 02

Partner Stock by TBS

The Brand retains ownership of the inventory while it is stored at TBS. TBS stores, manages, commercializes, delivers, invoices the B2B customer, and manages receivables.

✓ Included
  • Receipt and storage of Brand inventory
  • Inventory, SKU, lot, and availability control
  • Listing on the TBS platform and commercial channels
  • Commercial management of sales originated by TBS
  • Picking, packing, order preparation, and dispatch
  • TBS electronic invoicing to the B2B customer
  • Customer collections and receivables management
  • Periodic settlement of units actually sold
  • Shipments to the Brand’s own customers as a fulfillment service
✗ Not included
  • Upfront purchase of inventory by TBS
  • Transfer of ownership of unsold inventory to TBS
  • Automatic financing of promotions or trade marketing
  • Activations or POS materials unless specifically agreed
  • Special technology integrations unless specifically agreed
TBS is responsible for
  • Safeguarding inventory from confirmed receipt
  • Managing and reporting inventory balances and movements
  • Commercializing the products through its channels
  • Preparing and delivering orders sold by TBS
  • Invoicing the B2B customer and managing receivables
  • Assuming credit risk on sales invoiced by TBS
  • Settling the Brand for units actually sold
  • Losses or damage attributable to TBS operational custody
Brand is responsible for
  • Retaining ownership of unsold inventory
  • Providing compliant, saleable, documented product with sufficient shelf life
  • Replenishing inventory according to agreed levels
  • Defining transfer prices and commercial terms with TBS
  • Invoicing TBS only for units actually sold
  • Taking responsibility for quality, defects, recalls, and regulatory requirements
  • Funding promotions, activations, and discounts where applicable
Model rule: inventory stored at TBS does not constitute a purchase by TBS. The commercial transfer to TBS occurs only for units sold to B2B customers. For sales originated by TBS, TBS invoices the customer and manages receivables; for the Brand’s own customers, the Brand invoices and collects directly, while TBS provides the corresponding logistics service.
Model 03

Fulfillment by TBS

TBS operates logistics for the Brand. The Brand retains the commercial relationship with its customers, defines the sale, invoices, and collects directly.

✓ Included
  • Receipt of goods where applicable
  • Operational storage when agreed
  • Picking, packing, order staging, and preparation
  • Urban distribution in Cartagena and special deliveries
  • Proof-of-delivery management, traceability, exceptions, and returns
  • Operations for the Brand’s own customers
  • Operational reports on movements and dispatches
✗ Not included
  • Commercialization of the product by TBS
  • TBS invoicing to the end customer
  • Collections or receivables for the Brand’s customers
  • Purchase or consignment of inventory by TBS
  • Advertising, activations, or trade marketing (additional service)
TBS is responsible for
  • Executing the agreed logistics operation
  • Safeguarding product from receipt through delivery
  • Reporting movements, exceptions, and proofs of delivery
  • Losses or damage attributable to TBS operational custody
  • Promptly reporting exceptions that affect service
Brand is responsible for
  • Defining the sale and commercial relationship with its customer
  • Invoicing and collecting directly from the customer
  • Providing complete and timely dispatch instructions
  • Providing legally compliant, documented product suitable for transportation
  • Departmental excise requirements and other product-specific requirements
Important: Fulfillment by TBS does not involve TBS purchasing inventory or commercializing the product. The Brand retains the commercial relationship, invoices, and collects directly from the customer; TBS provides and invoices only the agreed logistics services.
Service catalog

Additional services

Any service not expressly included in the base model is considered an additional service and must be quoted and agreed separately.

Special logistics

Storage, cross-docking, urban distribution in Cartagena, urgent deliveries, reverse logistics, product destruction, and certified delivery.

Off-trade and retail chains

Preparation for supermarkets, liquor stores, and retail chains. Management of receiving requirements, barcodes, delivery windows, and distribution centers.

Value-added handling

Tax-stamp application/removal, repacking, corrugated packaging, promotional bundle assembly, shrink-wrapping, barcode printing, and promotional kits.

Trade marketing

Activations in bars, restaurants, and liquor stores; tastings, training, menu placements, service rituals, and Brand launches.

Platform advertising

Banners, featured products, featured Brand placement, Brand landing pages, premium product pages, internal search campaigns, coupons, and editorial content.

Data and technology

Sales reporting by city, channel, and SKU; sell-through, repeat purchase, and low-rotation alerts. API and EDI integrations and custom reporting.

General terms

Policies applicable
to all models

Core rules governing the operation regardless of the selected engagement model.

Compliant and saleable product

The Brand guarantees that all products comply with applicable legal, tax, sanitary, trademark, tax-stamp, and departmental excise requirements.

Availability and inventory

The Brand maintains accurate and up-to-date availability. Stockouts not reported in a timely manner are the Brand’s responsibility.

Pricing and terms

The Brand must communicate prices, discounts, and changes at least 30 days in advance. TBS is not responsible for discrepancies caused by outdated information.

Invoicing and supporting documents

Each model has its own invoicing mechanics. The Brand provides invoices, delivery notes, proofs of delivery, and any documents required to formalize the transactions.

Returns and exceptions

They are classified by cause: TBS error, Brand error, customer rejection, damage, defective product, or expired product. Responsibility depends on the cause and the selected model.

Product suspension

TBS may suspend a product listing due to recurring stockouts, logistics failures, incomplete documentation, legal risks, or low rotation without commercial action.
